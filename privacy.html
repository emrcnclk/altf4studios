Idle Pixel Hero – Privacy Policy

ALT F4 STUDIOS · Effective 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information Idle Pixel Hero (“the game”) handles, on Android, iOS, Windows and macOS, and who it is shared with. The short version: the game has no accounts of its own, runs no servers, and does not collect analytics. Your progress lives on your device. The only parties that can receive data about you are the platform services you use (Google, Apple, Valve) and, on phones, the advertising partner described below.

1. Data kept on your device

Your game progress, settings and a record of the purchases you have made are saved on your device. ALT F4 STUDIOS does not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Google Play Games (Android) and Game Center (iOS)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it to show your achievements and to keep a copy of your progress in your Google account (Saved Games), so you can continue on a new device. Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID and display name for this. On iOS, achievements are shared with Apple Game Center in the same way. These services are run by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies: Google, Apple. You can delete the saved progress from the Google Play Games app settings.

3. In-app purchases

Hero packs and “No Ads” are sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by them; we never see your payment details. The game receives a receipt confirming what you own and stores it on your device so your purchases keep working offline.

4. Advertising (Android and iOS)

The free phone versions show ads through Unity LevelPlay, which works with other ad networks to fill them. To show and measure ads, these partners may collect information from your device such as its advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and how you interact with the ads.

  • Personalised ads are off unless you turn them on. The “Personal ads” switch in the game's settings controls this, and you can change it at any time. With it off, ads are not personalised to you.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Buying “No Ads” stops the full-screen ads.

Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which also lists the ad networks it works with.

5. Notifications

On phones, the game can schedule one reminder on your device for when offline progress is full. It is created and delivered on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

6. Steam (Windows and macOS)

The desktop version uses Steam for achievements and to check which hero packs you own. This is handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or other personal information.
  • We do not run analytics or crash reporting.
  • We do not sell your data.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we hold no data about you ourselves, these requests are best made to the service that holds it (sections 2–6); we are happy to help you with that.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
